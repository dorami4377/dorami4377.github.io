Khichuri : A taste of Home

dorami4377September 11, 20261 min read

It was pouring outside. 

Arohi woke up at 7.30 am . Usually she woke up at 7 am but since  it was holiday , she slept a little longer. She was a working woman and lived alone in an apartment . She had to prepare herself breakfast but couldn’t decide what to cook. Suddenly an idea popped into her mind .

“ Khichuri.”

“ It will be perfect for today’s weather.”

At first, she freshened up . Then she went to the  kitchen and prepared everything. She started cutting the  vegetables and washing the rice and lentils . She put a pan on the stove and started cooking. 

Suddenly she remembered the old days. 

On a rainy day , her mother used to cook Khichuri for whole family. She and her brothers waited eagerly in the dining table. 

“ Ma , how long we have to wait ?? We are starving. ”

“ wait two minutes, dear .”

“ Ok ”

“ Here your Khichuri, enjoy ! . ”

Her mother served the Khichuri with fried brinjal , potatoes and many other side dishes. 

They loved it . 

“oh , delicious!”

“ Yummy !”

Arohi suddenly came back to reality.  

She served her plain Khichuri in a bowl and looked around at her empty dining space . The apartment was silent. Her  heart felt heavy. Tears unconsciously welled up in her eyes…..

She took a spoonful of Khichuri and smiled faintly. Somehow it didn’t taste the same anymore.
