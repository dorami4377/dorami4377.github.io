Secret admirer

Can’t you read my eyes? Do you even know that I exist in your world? Am I invisible to you?

Is it my fault that I’m not expressive? Don’t my eyes tell you what I want so badly? Maybe you need lenses to read my eyes.

Secret admirer “

Every day, I write a letter to him and keep it instead of sending it.

Maybe one day, I’ll actually have the courage to give it to him.

But not today.

“Sri, Sri, Sriii.”

“Yes, Miss.”

My teacher looked at me annoyingly.

“What are you doing in class? Pay attention to the lecture.”

I became nervous and quickly hid the letter inside my notebook.

“Okay, Miss.”

Dhruv looked at me and then laughed.

Why do I always become such a fool in front of him?

Maybe he thinks I’m dumb.

Sigh.

“That’s all for today. Thank you. Rai, collect the homework and put it on my desk.”

“Yes, Miss.”

Everyone became busy packing their things.

Dhruv was sitting on the bench, playing the guitar.

Every note he played sent butterflies fluttering through my stomach.

Why does everything he does affect me so much?

“Give me your homework, Sri,” Rai said.

“Oh.”

I handed my notebook to Rai without giving it much thought.

And then—

“Everyone, pay attention to me! Look what I found in class!”

Rockey’s voice suddenly filled the room.

The whole class went silent.

Something felt wrong.

I looked up.

And there it was.

My letter!!

In Rockey’s hand.

For a moment, my mind went completely blank.

Before Rockey could say anything about it, I rushed toward him.

But it was too late.

He grinned.

“It’s a love letter for Dhruv.”

My heart stopped.

Before I could do anything, Rockey started reciting it aloud.

Every word.

Every embarrassing confession.

Every feeling I had secretly poured onto that piece of paper.

I felt my face burning.

I couldn’t bear it anymore.

I turned around and ran straight to the washroom.

I didn’t know how I was going to face Dhruv now.

All I wanted was to disappear.

From that day on, I started avoiding him.

No one knew that I had written the letter.

But somehow, that didn’t make it any easier.

I still didn’t have the courage to face him.

Whenever I encountered him, I just ran away.

Maybe I was trying to distance myself from him.

Or maybe…

I was trying to hide from my own feelings.

“Hey, Sri.”

“Hm?”

I turned around.

What?! Dhruv?!

What should I do now?

I immediately tried to walk away, but he stopped me.

“Why are you avoiding me?”

“No, I’m not.”

“Then why do you run away whenever you see me?”

I fell silent.

He looked at me for a moment before asking,

“Are you free this Friday?”

“Mm… yes.”

“Okay, then. We can do our homework together.”

I stared at him.

Am I dreaming, or is this real?

If this was a dream, I didn’t want to wake up.

**Dhruv’s POV: **

15 July

I saw a girl with a bright smile playing with a puppy on campus.

Her eyes had a different kind of sparkle.

I had never seen an aura like hers before.

There was something about her.

Something I couldn’t explain.

Her cute little actions were melting my heart.

How could someone be this cute?

Sri Roy.

Every time I saw her in the classroom, I wanted to get her attention.

And somehow, I always ended up acting stupid.

I don’t know why, but around her, I wanted to show my childish side.

She always looked so playful.

Seeing her smile made my day.

She was like a rose protected by thorns—beautiful, but somehow impossible to get close to.

Whenever our eyes met in the classroom, even for a few seconds, I just wanted to stop time.

But I never had the courage to look at her directly.

Instead, I searched for excuses to look at her.

Every little thing she did caught my attention.

And every time she tied her hair…

My whole system crashed.

On cultural night, she wore a blue saree with a bindi.

That was enough to drive me crazy.

In my mind, the song Perfect started playing.

When I saw you in that dress Looking so beautiful I don’t deserve this Darling, you look perfect tonight.

Honestly, I didn’t think I deserved her.

She was like the moon—beautiful, distant, and impossible to reach.

Thoughts of her played in my mind nonstop.

I melted every time I thought about her.

When Rockey started reciting the letter, I noticed Sri’s expression.

She looked completely stressed.

At first, I took the letter as a joke.

But as soon as Sri left the classroom, a thought struck me.

Maybe…

No.

What am I thinking?

It couldn’t be possible.

And yet…

I wanted to believe that maybe she liked me too.

I took the letter from Rockey.

Then I started comparing the handwriting.

My heart began racing.

I looked at the letter again.

Then at the handwriting.

And suddenly, everything made sense.

“It’s really you, Sri.”

My eyes filled with joy.

I had never imagined this.

The girl I secretly liked…

The girl I couldn’t even look at properly…

Maybe she felt the same way about me.

I couldn’t sleep that night.

I kept thinking about her.

Now I couldn’t wait to talk to her.

Everyone knew how I felt except her.

And now…

I couldn’t wait for tomorrow.

But the next day, she started avoiding me.

I felt bad.

Still, I convinced myself that she was probably just embarrassed.

So I decided to take the initiative.

I would go and talk to her myself.

And that’s exactly what I did.

One day

“Hey, Sri.”

“Hm?”

She turned around.

The moment her eyes met mine, I noticed the slight panic on her face.

There it is again.

That same expression she had every time she saw me lately.

She immediately tried to walk away, but I stopped her.

“Why are you avoiding me?”

“No, I’m not.”

I couldn’t help but smile a little.

“Then why do you run away whenever you see me?”

She suddenly fell silent.

She looked down, avoiding my eyes.

I wanted to ask her about the letter. I wanted to tell her that I knew it was hers.

But somehow, I couldn’t bring myself to say it.

Instead, I looked at her for a moment and asked,

“Are you free this Friday?”

She looked at me, a little surprised.

“Mm… yes.”

I smiled.

“Okay, then. We can do our homework together.”

She nodded quietly.

I tried to act normal, but inside, my heart was celebrating.

She said yes.

She had no idea that homework was the last thing on my mind.

I just wanted an excuse to spend some time with her.

And maybe…

this Friday would finally give me the courage to get a little closer to my secret admirer.

That was only the beginning.
