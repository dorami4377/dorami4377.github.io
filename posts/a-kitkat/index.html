A KitKat

dorami4377September 12, 20261 min read

Ruby is an 8-year-old schoolgirl. She wears spectacles to see clearly. One day, she returned home early and brought a KitKat. She wanted to find her brother and share it with him.

She looked around the room, but she couldn't find him. Confused, she walked to the window and looked outside.

There he was!

She went to the garden, where her brother, Tom, was playing. His hands were covered with soil. Seeing this, she became angry and shouted at him.

She grabbed his hand and took him to the bathroom. Tom looked at her innocently.

Ruby carefully cleaned his dirty hands. After that, her anger slowly disappeared. Then, she gently stroked her little brother's hair.

She smiled and gave him a KitKat.

Sometimes, love looks like anger first—but it is still love.
