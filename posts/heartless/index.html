Heartless

Chapter 1 — Meeting You

Everyone was busy celebrating the New Year, but as always, I found myself sitting alone in a corner. I've never liked social gatherings. Being surrounded by too many people always made me feel suffocated. But this time, I had another reason to feel sad in the middle of such a happy celebration.

My family was moving soon.

Even though I didn't want to leave, I had tried my best to convince my parents to stay in New York. Every time mom would tell me,

“Milly, don't be upset. You should be happy about this. Your dad got promoted, and I'm sure you'll make lots of new friends there.”

But making friends wasn't easy for me. I wasn't an outgoing person.

This town held some of my most beautiful memories. The familiar streets, the people, and everything I had grown used to—soon, I would have to leave them all behind. Tonight, even the bright fireworks couldn't lift my mood. 

Maybe a new chapter of my life was about to begin. I closed my eyes. 

“Spare me... don't do this. Please... please!” I woke up with a start. My heart was racing, and my entire body felt cold. It was just a nightmare. At least, that's what I wished it was.But the truth was far more painful. It wasn't just a nightmare. It was a dark chapter of my life—one I desperately wished I could forget.

“Milly! Wake up! It's already 8:30! Get up now, otherwise you'll be late!”

“Mom, I'm already awake,” I replied sleepily.

Today was my first day at my new school.  As I got ready, I could feel butterflies in my stomach. At breakfast, Dad looked at me and smiled.

“That uniform really suits you.”

Mom placed a plate of garlic toast in front of me.

“Eat properly, Milly.”

I smiled. My parents had been married  for nearly seventeen years, yet somehow they still cared about each other like newlyweds. After breakfast, I grabbed my bag and headed to the bus stop.

 

I stopped at the school gate . I took a deep breath.

A new school.

A new place.

A new beginning

.

When I entered the classroom, I stood at the door nervously.

“May I come in, Miss?”

The teacher looked at me and smiled.

“Yes, come in.”

I walked to the last bench and quietly took my seat.

Everyone seemed to know each other.

They were talking, laughing, and joking around.

I remained silent.

Then someone suddenly asked,

“What's your name?”

“Milly.”

That was all I said.

At lunchtime, I noticed a boy playing basketball on the field. I don't know why, but something about him caught my attention. I quickly looked away. I didn't know why but I felt a strange vibe about him. 

Chapter 2 — Noticing You

I couldn't stand him. He was so annoying. I liked quiet environments, but Peter seemed to be the complete opposite. Whenever he asked questions in class, I found myself getting irritated.

He always seemed so confident. Sometimes, he acted as if he knew everything.

“Hi, Milly.”

I looked up. It was him . 

“How are you doing?”

“Mmm , fine . But how..”

He was about to say something when someone called from across the classroom,

“Peter! Come here!”

That's when I learned his name.

Peter.

He was popular. He seemed to have a lot of friends.

After that day, I don't know what happened to me. Maybe I should have talked to him instead of constantly thinking about him. I started noticing little things about him. The way he talked. The way he laughed. The way he raised his hand in class.

Somehow, he was constantly on my mind.

One day, he asked,

“Are you coming to Mark's birthday party , Milly ?”

Then he looked directly into my eyes. For a moment, my mind went completely blank.  I panicked and answered, 

 “ no no , I’m busy ”

I walked away before he could say anything else. Every time he called my name, my heart skipped a beat. I kept telling myself—

It's not love. 

It's just a strange feeling. 

Why was I running away from him? And why did he seem to be everywhere?

“Milly!”

I turned around. It was Juliana. Juli has been my only friend here since I was here . 

She was bold, confident, and completely different from me. She had short brown hair and sharp eyes, and she was never afraid to say what was on her mind. We went to the cafeteria together.

The cafeteria's environment was calm but it was calm before the storm. 

Then suddenly—

Someone bumped into me.I lost my balance. Before I could fall, someone caught me around my waist. I froze. Blue jeans. A hoodie. Messy hair. Glasses. Peter. It was him 

“Are you okay?” he asked.

I couldn't think. I couldn't breathe. Then—

Slap!

 I slapped him. I ran away. 

“Milly! Wait!”

I could hear Juli shouting behind me.  Peter was speechless. He was standing there like a statue. 

Later, at the football ground, Juli looked at me.

“Why did you do that?”

“I don't know.”

“You slapped him!”

“It was a reflex.”

She sighed.

“You should apologize.”

 I took a day off because I couldn’t face him . When I got home, Mom looked surprised.

“Why are you home so early?”

“I missed home.”

But the incident kept replaying in my mind. He had only tried to help me. I should apologize. I opened Instagram. There was already a message from Peter.

Peter:

Sorry if I offended you, but I only wanted to save you.

I stared at the message for a few seconds.

Then I replied.

Me:

No. I'm sorry.

A moment later, I added:

I'm sorry... and thank you for helping me.

His reply came quickly.

Peter:

It's my pleasure.

I smiled.

Maybe...

He wasn't as annoying as I thought.

Chapter 3 — One Step Closer 

It was 9 a.m., and I was getting ready to leave for school.

As soon as I stepped outside, I froze.

Peter was standing in front of my house.

“Hi! Good morning, Milly.”

I stared at him.

“What are you doing here?”

He looked away awkwardly.

“I was... just passing nearby. So so … 

Aren’t we late for school ??”

“Oh.”

We started walking. It was a foggy January morning. The streets were unusually quiet. I secretly glanced at him. But he was already looking at me. Our eyes met. I immediately looked away.

Every time I looked into his eyes, my mind went blank. I'd never felt anything like this before.

When we reached the bus station, the bus arrived.

It was crowded. Peter looked around and suddenly pointed toward an empty seat.

“Milly, here.”

I sat down.He remained standing in front of me. The bus was packed with people , but Peter stood there, shielding me from the crowd. I hadn't the courage to look at his face. Instead, my eyes fell on his name badge.

Peter.

For some reason, seeing his name made me smile. When we arrived at school, we met Juli.

“Hi, dude! So, you two sorted everything out?”

Peter smiled.

“Hmm. Isn't that right?”

“Yes,” I replied.

Juli looked between us suspiciously .

 Peter , “I have something to do. I'll see you later.”

He walked away.

Later, she asked me,

“What's going on between you two?”

“Nothing.”

“Hmm.”

Before she could say anything else, the teacher entered.

“Good morning, students.”

“Good morning, Miss.”

The teacher placed some papers on the desk.

“Today, I'm assigning you a group project on Human Behavior.”

She started announcing the groups.

Then—

“Milly, Peter, and Juliana.”

I looked at Peter.

He smiled.

“ See Mily . destiny wants us  to be together ”

Chapter 4 — Butterflies in My Stomach

“Milly, where are you? I'm already on my way!”

“I'm coming!”

I hurried outside. Juli was waiting for me near the café. I was wearing a yellow floral dress.

When I reached the café, Peter was already there. He looked at me and froze for a moment.

“You look... different today.”

I smiled awkwardly.

“Different?”

“Beautiful.”

My cheeks immediately turned warm.

Juli looked between us and grinned.

“Ohhh...”

“Stop it, Juli.”

We went inside the café and sat near the window. We started working on our Human Behavior assignment. We gathered information, discussed ideas, and wrote everything down.

At lunchtime, a staff member came over.

“Would you like to order something?”

Peter asked us what we would like to have . I said 

“ a cold coffee and a vanilla pastry.” 

Juli said the same . But the staff member replied that they had run out of vanilla pastries. My mood immediately dropped. Then Peter asked if I wanted to order anything else . 

“ No , it’s ok ”

“ then just three cold coffees , ”

We continued working until we finally finished the assignment.

By then, it was already getting dark.

“We should leave, Peter, can you drop Mily, ” Juli said.

“ yes ”

Outside, we went our separate ways. Peter and I walked together. He asked me a few questions.I answered quietly. Then he asked,

“Why don't you talk much?”

I looked at him.

“I can't open up easily.”

He nodded.

“That's okay.”

He smiled gently.

“Take your time, Milly.”

The cold wind blew against my face.

Peter suddenly stopped. Before I could ask why, he took off his muffler and wrapped it around my neck.

“Peter...”

“You're cold.”

“Thank you.”

“I don't mind.”

We continued walking. When we reached near my house, I stopped.

“Good night. Message me after you reach home . ”

“Good night, Milly.”

He waited until I entered my house. Later that night, my phone buzzed.

Peter:

I reached home safely. Good night. See you tomorrow.

Then another message appeared.

Peter:

And Milly... you look beautiful when you smile.

I stared at the screen. My cheeks became warm.I smiled. Something was changing.Something inside me. And I was beginning to realize...

Maybe…,

Chapter 5 — Falling

The next morning, I brought Peter's muffler back to school.

“Here.”

He smiled.

“Thanks.”

Before I could say anything else, he handed me a small box.

I opened it. A vanilla pastry. I looked at him.

“Why?”

“Because ...”

Juli immediately started teasing me.

“Oh my God!”

“Juli!”

She laughed.

A few moments later, Peter pulled something from his bag.

amusement park passes.

“Are you free next Sunday?”

I looked at him.

“An amusement park?”

He nodded.

“Would you guys come with me?”

Before I could answer, Juli interrupted.

“Ok ,”

Smiled innocently. 

The following Sunday arrived.

Juli cancelled at the last moment because she wasn't feeling well. So Peter and I went alone.

The amusement park was crowded and full of lights, laughter, and music.

For the first time in a long time, I felt completely free. We went on different rides. We laughed.We talked.

At one point, someone accidentally bumped into me in the crowd.

Peter immediately stepped closer.

“Are you okay?”

I nodded.

“I'm fine.”

He smiled.  I felt safe when I was with him . 

Later, we sat together and watched the lights around the park.

I looked at him. He looked back. There was something different in his eyes.

Something serious. When we finally reached home, he stopped in front of me.

“Milly?”

“Hmm?”

“If I asked you to go out with me... would you?”

My heart started beating faster.

I looked down.

“Maybe... I would.”

He smiled.

Then, unexpectedly, he got down on one knee.

My eyes widened.

“Milly Carter...”

He held out his hand.

“Will you be my first frost?”

Snow began to fall softly around us.

For a moment, I couldn't say anything.

Then I smiled.

“Yes.”

Chapter 6 — A New Beginning

Our dating era had officially begun. I told Juli about our relationship the next day.

“Whattt? You two are dating?”

I couldn't help but smile.

“I knew it!” she exclaimed. “I'm so happy for you, Milly.”

I looked down, trying to hide my smile.

“I don't know why I accepted him,” I murmured. “But somehow... I know I can trust him.”

Juli smiled.

“Hmm. I can see that.”

Our relationship was quite private. Not many people knew about us, and honestly, I liked it that way. It felt like our own little world. No gossip. No unnecessary attention. Just us.

One day, Peter took me to the riverside. It was quiet and peaceful, with very few people around.

He had chosen the place himself. We went boating together. The gentle sound of the water, the cool breeze against my face, and Peter sitting beside me made me forget everything else.

For once, my mind was quiet. I was happy. Really happy.

When we got off the boat, Peter stepped onto the shore first. Then he turned around and held out his hand. I looked at it for a moment. Then I placed my hand in his without hesitation. His fingers closed gently around mine. And somehow, in that simple moment, I realized something.

He was someone I could rely on. We walked over to a nearby bench and sat down.

After a while, I rested my head against his shoulder.

The silence between us felt comfortable.

Then I whispered,

“Do you know something?”

“Hmm?”

“I feel safe with you.”

He turned slightly toward me.

“You're the only man I've ever felt safe with.”

My voice trembled.

“You're the only man I...”

I stopped. Peter didn't rush me. He simply waited. I took a deep breath.

“When I was younger... I was sexually abused by my uncle.”

The words felt heavy as they left my mouth.

“I was terrified after that. I started hating being touched. Whenever someone came too close to me, I felt uncomfortable and uneasy.”

My eyes filled with tears.

“I isolated myself. I stopped trusting people. I thought I would always feel this way.”

Peter remained silent. He listened to every word. He didn't interrupt. He didn't ask unnecessary questions. He simply reached for me and pulled me into a gentle hug. I broke down in his arms.He wiped away my tears and slowly stroked my hair.

“I can't change your past, Milly,” he whispered.

I looked up at him.

“But I can try to make your present so beautiful that, one day, your past won't hurt as much.”

His words were warm. For the first time, I felt like someone truly understood me. I closed my eyes. And held him a little tighter.

Maybe… this really was a new beginning.

Chapter 7 — Betrayal

I was sitting in the classroom when I suddenly noticed something strange.

People were whispering around me.  Their eyes kept moving toward me.

I frowned.

“Isn't she...?”

“It's Milly.”

“Did you see the post?”

My heart began to beat faster. Before I could understand what was happening, Juli rushed into the classroom. She looked terrified.

“Milly, are you okay?”

“I'm fine. But why? What's going on?”

She hesitated.

“I'll tell you, but promise me you won't panic.”

“Juli, just tell me.”

She slowly held out her phone.

“Look.”

I took it.  And everything inside me stopped.

A post had been published on the student forum.

BREAKING NEWS: New Transfer Student Revealed as a Sexual Assault Survivor.

Below it were details about my past.

My uncle.  The abuse. Everything I had tried so hard to bury. Everything I had trusted Peter enough to tell him. It was all there. My hands started shaking.

My whole universe scattered in a matter of seconds.

Someone had taken the darkest part of my life and turned it into gossip.

A hashtag with my name was already spreading.

#Milly

I couldn't breathe. I couldn't think. The only person I wanted to see was Peter.

I ran through the hallway looking for him.  And then I saw him.

Peter was standing with Conrad. I stopped.

Before I could call his name, I heard him speak.

“I wanted revenge on her.”

My heart shattered. He continued talking.

“She slapped me that day. I thought I'd make her pay for it.”

I couldn't hear anything after that.

No.

Please, no.

The person I had trusted with my deepest secret… The person who had made me feel safe… Had he really done this?  My tears welled up. I turned around and ran. I didn't let him see me. I didn't let him explain. I didn't want to hear another word.

I rushed back to the classroom, grabbed my bag, and left the campus. I felt numb. When I reached home, I went straight to my room. I threw my bag onto the floor and rushed into the bathroom.

The moment the shower started, I broke down.

“How could he?”

My voice trembled.

“Why?”

Tears mixed with the water running down my face.

“Why, God?”

I covered my face with my hands.

“Why do I always have to suffer?”

My eyes became red from crying.

I had trusted him.

I had finally allowed myself to love someone.

And somehow...

the world had taken that away from me too.

Chapter 8 — Goodbye

I didn’t know how many days had passed. I stopped checking my phone. I stopped going to school. I barely spoke to anyone. I just stayed in my room, trying to disappear from the world.

Then, one afternoon, Juli came to visit me.

She knocked softly on my door.

“Milly?”

I opened it. The moment I saw her, I hugged her. Tears immediately rolled down my cheeks.

“Why did he do this to me?”

Juli pulled away and looked at me.

“Who?”

“Peter.”

She stared at me in disbelief.

“Wait. No.”

Her voice became firm.

“No, Milly. That's not true.”

I looked at her through my tears.

“What?”

“The post was made by someone from the senior group.”

I couldn't speak.

“Peter found out,” Juli continued. “He confronted him. He even fought him and made sure the post was deleted.”

My heart started pounding.

“He... what?”

“He's in the hospital now.”

I stared at her. For a moment, I couldn't breathe. Then I grabbed my phone. I turned it on for the first time in days. Thousands of notifications flooded my screen.

Messages.

Missed calls.

Emails.

All from Peter.

My fingers trembled as I opened them.

Milly, are you okay?

Please answer me.

I'm sorry.

Please don't think I did this.

I didn't do anything.

Please believe me.

There were so many messages. And then I found one that made my heart ache.

“At first, I wanted to take revenge on you because of what happened that day. But somewhere along the way, I started liking you for real. I started loving you. I never meant to hurt you. I love you, Milly.”

My eyes filled with tears. He had been telling the truth. And I hadn't even given him a chance to explain. I stood up suddenly.

“I have to go.”

“Milly—”

But I was already running. I ran toward the hospital. My eyes were filled with tears, and my heart was pounding. My phone was still in my hand. I called him. The phone rang.

Then I heard his voice.

“Milly?”

“Peter...”

My voice broke.

“I'm sorry.”

There was silence on the other end.

“Please come back,” he whispered.

“I am.”

I wiped my tears.

“I'm coming to you.”

I ran faster. When Peter heard that I was coming, he hurried downstairs.

The moment he stepped into the ground-floor entrance, he saw me. We stood facing each other.

For a few seconds, neither of us moved. There were people around us, but I couldn't see anyone. I could only see him.

Peter.

The person I had loved.

The person I had lost.

The person I had finally found again.

“Peter...”

His eyes filled with tears.

“Milly.”

I couldn't stop myself.

I ran toward him.

“Milly, wait!”

But I didn't hear him. I was already crossing the road.

Suddenly, Peter's eyes widened.

“Milly!”

A bus was approaching. Everything happened in a second

.

“Milly, STOP!”

I turned toward him. Our eyes met.

And then—

Everything went silent. A loud horn. A sudden scream. And then darkness.

“MILLYYYYY!”

Peter's voice echoed through the street. He ran toward her. His world had just fallen apart.

The girl he had finally found… The girl he had promised to protect… Was gone.

And all he could do was hold her and cry.

“I'm sorry, Milly...”

His voice broke.

“I'm so sorry.”

He held her tightly, as if refusing to accept what had happened. But sometimes...

Love finds us at the wrong time.

And sometimes...

the people we finally learn to trust are the ones we have to say goodbye to.

The End.
